A Legacy of Love: 10 Years of Shih Tzu Excellence

What began as my mother's dream has become my life's mission – raising the healthiest, happiest Shih Tzu puppies in a warm family environment.

Carolina Delsa
Second‑generation breeder

From Mum's Kitchen to a Premier Kennel

Over a decade ago, my mother raised her first Shih Tzu litter in our cozy home. I grew up surrounded by wagging tails and puppy kisses. When she retired, she entrusted me with her legacy – the ethics, the love, and the commitment to producing puppies that are not just beautiful but truly healthy and well‑tempered.

Today, I, Carolina Delsa, continue her work with over 10 years of hands‑on experience. Every puppy is born in our living room, not a kennel. They are handled daily by children and adults, ensuring they are confident and ready for family life.
