Welcome to Delasa Forever Shih Tzu, your trusted source for healthy, well-bred Shih Tzu puppies. As a reputable Shih Tzu breeder with over 10 years of experience, I am committed to raising puppies that are not only beautiful but also healthy, well-tempered, and properly socialized.

Why Shih Tzu Puppies Make Wonderful Family Pets

Shih Tzus are one of the most beloved toy breeds in North America. Known for their affectionate nature, playful personality, and loyal companionship, Shih Tzus make excellent family dogs. They adapt well to apartment living, are great with children when properly socialized, and thrive on human companionship.

Our Commitment to Ethical Breeding

At Delasa Forever Shih Tzu, I follow strict ethical breeding practices. All my breeding dogs undergo comprehensive health testing including OFA patella evaluations, CERF eye examinations, cardiac screenings, and DNA testing for hereditary conditions.
